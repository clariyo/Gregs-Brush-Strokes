The Greg's Brush Strokes approach

An eye for the work most people never notice — until it isn't there.

A flawless topcoat only holds up when the work beneath it has been done with the same care. Greg treats preparation as the real job — filling, sanding, priming, caulking every junction — so the finish is clean, the lines are true, and the paint goes the distance.

  • Surfaces prepared thoroughly before a brush is loaded
  • Floors, furniture and fittings protected throughout
  • Materials chosen to suit the room, the surface and the wear
  • A tidy site every day, and a spotless one at the end
